Availability label reads QA (user's answer), and only GitHub is a real link.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a press proof: the stage where work is checked before it goes to press. It refuses the category default (centred hero over a uniform card grid) and instead shows Jay's work the way a printer shows a job for approval: plates framed in crop marks, every claim tied to a figure.

OWN-WORLD: Uncoated warm stock ground, process-black ink, two cool grays, and one spot colour, process cyan, used only for registration marks, the active indicator, focus, and misregistration. A Swiss grotesk (Schibsted Grotesk) carries everything from 0.8rem to 6rem. A Helvetica-cut mono (Fragment Mono) is used only for slug lines, figure numbers, measurements, and code. Hairline rules, L-shaped crop marks, a registration target, and FPO-crossed boxes for missing imagery. No radius, no shadows.

STORY: The visitor sees the claim (software that checks its work before it acts), watches LikeFlow's real state machine prove it, opens the case study, scans capabilities tied to evidence, and writes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slug-line nav at the top. Below it, a wide headline across 9 of 12 columns whose cyan plate slides into register on load. The intro and two CTAs sit in a narrow left column under it. To the right, Plate 01 (LikeFlow's live status card, rebuilt in HTML) straddles the fold inside crop marks. A colour-bar scroll cue sits bottom-left.

FORM: Press proof sheet, candidate 7 of 7 on the ordered list. seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: the headline coming into register, plus the live LikeFlow status loop (pausable). Motion grammar: a single register settle, expo-out.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
